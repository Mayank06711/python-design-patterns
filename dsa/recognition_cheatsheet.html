§ The "Heap vs Monotonic Deque" Confusion

Common mistake: confusing "top k" (heap) with "max in window of size k" (monotonic deque). They look similar because both say "k".

The difference is:
  • Top-K — no window, no sliding. Look at ALL elements, pick the k best. → heap
  • Sliding window extremum — there's a window of size k that slides across the array. Elements enter and leave in a fixed order. → monotonic deque

The tell: does the word "window" or "every subarray of size k" appear? If yes → monotonic deque. If no → heap.
